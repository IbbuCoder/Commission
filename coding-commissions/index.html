faq

          
Questions, answered

          
Don't see yours? Ask in the project description and I'll answer when I reply.

        

        

          

            What can you code?
            
Almost anything related to programming and technology, including websites, bots, scripts, automation, software, APIs, AI integrations, Minecraft/server projects, and more.

          

          

            What languages do you work with?
            
Python, C++, Rust, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, C#, Go, PHP, Lua, Luau, HTML, CSS, SQL, and many others depending on the project.

          

          

            What if my project isn't listed?
            
Ask anyway. The services listed are examples, not limitations.

          

          

            How does the Instant Estimate work?
            
The quiz uses the information you provide to calculate an initial estimated price range.

          

          

            Is the Instant Estimate the final price?
            
No. It is an automated preliminary estimate. The final quote is determined after reviewing the complete project requirements.

          

          

            Will my answers be sent to you?
            
Yes. After submission, the complete quiz response, including your contact information, project details, and preliminary estimate, is automatically sent for review.

          

          

            When will I hear back?
            
I'll review the submitted project and respond by email within 1–2 days.

          

          

            Can you work on existing code?
            
Yes. Existing projects can be debugged, modified, expanded, or improved depending on the codebase.
